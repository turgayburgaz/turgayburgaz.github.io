Privacy Policy – youtube-icerik

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who uses this app

youtube-icerik is a private tool used only by its owner, Turgay Burgaz, to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's own YouTube account to upload and schedule videos, add subtitles and translated titles/descriptions, and read channel and video statistics (for example views, likes and watch time) through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to analyse the performance of the owner's channels. Access tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or Google security settings. The owner can delete locally stored tokens and data at any time.
